THE PROBLEM

Small restaurants run on WhatsApp, paper and memory.

Indonesia counts 5.28 million F&B service businesses (BPS, 2024), yet most owners manage purchasing, stock and suppliers by hand — through invoices, WhatsApp chats, spreadsheets, or physical market visits. With no dedicated procurement team, records stay scattered and decisions go unrecorded.

Procurement, Inventory & Supplier Management

Owners struggle to track stock levels, detect supplier price changes, compare suppliers, and decide what and when to purchase. The result: overstocking, stockouts, and unnecessary costs — and when a price spikes, nothing proves when it started.

Sales & Menu Management

Owners may have sales records but lack the time or technical skills to analyze them in Excel. They need simple answers — which menus sell best, where sales are declining, what ingredients cost, what's actually profitable — not another complex spreadsheet.

“One owner we interviewed tracks daily totals in Excel — never per item, never per stock count. Not every purchase has a receipt. She prefers texting, and wants alerts when stock runs low and prices jump.”

From our field interviews with restaurant owners — the findings RestoPilot is built on.

THE SOLUTION

Keep your POS. RestoPilot owns the money-out.

POS systems and QRIS already own money-in — customer sales. RestoPilot captures the other half: what was bought, from whom, at what unit price, what stock remains, and who is owed. A WhatsApp AI agent plus a simple web dashboard, built for messy real-world inputs.

WhatsApp-first intake

Send purchase text, receipt photos, or voice notes — the way owners already communicate. Informal Indonesian is parsed into a structured draft: items, quantities, units, prices, supplier.

Approval before anything records

Nothing posts without the owner. Review and correct the draft on WhatsApp or the dashboard, then approve with a correlated YA. Missing or ambiguous fields are never silently guessed.

One atomic transaction

Approval records the purchase, the stock movement, and the audit trail in a single transaction. The dashboard, the WhatsApp summary and the activity log always report the same truth.

Dashboard truth & price evidence

Purchases, inventory estimates, pending approvals and activity in one view. Same-supplier 30-day price comparisons surface spikes — like the Telur +16.3% case — straight from your own approved history.

WHY IT'S SAFE

Honest by design.

RestoPilot handles financial records, so it is deliberately narrow and deliberately human-in-the-loop:

AI proposes; code calculates; a human decides.Unit-price math, unit conversion, price comparison, stock ledger and approval workflow are deterministic code — never LLM-decided.
Approval is correlated, never generic.A “YA” approves only the action explicitly tied to that conversation — never an unrelated pending draft. Stale versions, duplicates and cross-tenant decisions are rejected.
Every restaurant is an isolated tenant.A WhatsApp number is linked to exactly one restaurant. A registered number's conversation is scoped to its own restaurant, always.
Honesty over impressive numbers.Stock is an estimate (no POS consumption data yet). A recorded purchase is not a recorded payment. Comparisons come only from your own approved history.

QUESTIONS

Asked by owners, answered honestly.

Do I have to replace my POS or QRIS?

No. POS and QRIS keep owning money-in. RestoPilot only handles the money-out side: purchases, stock estimates and supplier prices. You keep every tool you already use.

What exactly can I send?

A WhatsApp or Telegram text like “beli cabai 3 kg 150rb”, a photo of a market nota, a PDF invoice, or a voice note. Photos and voice are transcribed into the same editable draft — you approve before anything records.

Can the AI spend or record money by itself?

Never. The model only reads and phrases; all math is deterministic code, and every write needs your explicit, correlated approval. A stale or duplicated “YA” is rejected.

Is my restaurant's data visible to others?

No. Each restaurant is an isolated tenant. A linked number's drafts, approvals and summaries are scoped to that restaurant only — the assistant cannot read or mention another restaurant's data.

How accurate is the stock estimate?

Stock starts from your approved purchases and improves with usage, waste and sales records — and a physical count (stok opname) resets it to ground truth whenever you take one. We say plainly: it is an estimate, and every correction is audited.

What does the demo include?

A seeded restaurant with purchase history, a WhatsApp simulator, the full dashboard, and both languages. Nothing you approve in the demo touches a real supplier — demo-grade authentication applies.
